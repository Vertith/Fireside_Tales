# Emit Time — Story Arc (main loop and true ending)

As it stands on 13 Oct 2026. It combines the story bible and what's actually in the live game (`game/js/content/`). Emit's two winning endings are summarised at the end. Other alternate endings are left out.

## The setup

A stormy night at Science Corp, a 90s research lab. You're the night security guard at the front desk. Every time lightning strikes, the hair on the back of your neck stands up.

## Prologue (about 10:15 to 10:51 PM)

1. **The first flash.** A huge white flash fills the lobby. The guard tells himself it was lightning. It wasn't: this is Emit arriving from the future. On later loops the narration changes to *"It wasn't lightning. It never was."*
2. **"Kane" runs in.** A tall man with wild grey hair, wearing a lab coat with a KANE badge, says he's just cracked time travel on the fourth floor. Then he runs back upstairs.
3. **The second flash.** About seventeen minutes later, a seven-foot man in glowing armor demands directions to the lab and says everyone inside will be terminated.
   - If you let him through, the loop resets.
   - If you shoot him, "Kane" comes down.
4. **The briefing.** "Kane" explains that now time travel exists, people from every era will come to stop it or steal it. He gives you a list of tonight's real visitors (government, researchers, company people) and tells you to screen everyone without letting them know you're onto them. He says no police. When asked why the guard, he says *"Because you're here. You're always here."* He tells you to clean up the mess.

## The night (10:52 PM to 6:04 AM, 30 visitors)

Each visitor gets three opening questions (name, what they want, how they got here). More questions unlock as you inspect things on them or remember them from earlier loops. Your actions are Let through, Turn away and Shoot, plus the shutdown device once you have it. Any wrong call sends you back to the first flash. The notebook, and any lines you've heard before (which you can now skip), carry across loops.

- **Act One, "The night shift"** (6 visitors, easy). Pizza driver, the 1920s gentleman, Kane's sister Ellen, the courier with a smartphone, Walt the janitor, the influencer.
  - **Key clue:** Ellen describes her brother, the real Kane, as short and bald with a grey moustache. That's not the man who ran in.
- **Act Two, "Company"** (10 visitors). DOE official, Victorian doctor, Channel 7 reporter, intern, monk, MIT researcher, WWII soldier, military liaison, hippie, Viking cosplayer.
  - **Interlude, 2:30 AM:** "Kane" gives you the **shutdown device** for anyone who "isn't a person". He says he built it tonight, but it looks years old.
- **Act Three, "Things that aren't people"** (10 visitors, androids appear). Android inspector, a lab tech's wife, emeritus professor, Roman "consultant", investor, food inspector, senator's aide, delivery robot operator, dot-com founder, Kane's mother.
  - **Interlude, 4:52 AM, "hold them off a bit longer":** he says he's nearly done and is carrying folders marked KANE, A. to burn. He tells you to let Lindqvist from Kessler Dynamics up at 5:30.
  - On loop 2 or later he slips and mentions how you handled the monk "this time", which shows he remembers the loops.
  - If you point the device at him, he flinches and asks where you got it, even though he gave it to you.
- **Act Four, "Wanted"** (5 visitors, hardest). Insurance rep, patent lawyer, then **Lindqvist the engineer**, who drops a **wanted poster: "WANTED: EMIT. Prisoner 0-1. Escaped custody, 2240."** After him come "future you" and **Warden Hask**. The warden shows you **Emit's prisoner file**. He explains that Emit has been intercepting visitors who would become a problem for him later, and "dealing" with them.
- **The pitch, 6:04 AM.** "Kane" comes down with nothing in his hands. He says the only way to stop this is to destroy time travel, and asks you to hold the desk for ten minutes. This is true but not the whole story. Destroying it is his fallback, because then nobody can hunt him. What he really wants is to control time travel himself. If you shoot him here, or call him Emit, he touches his watch and the night snaps back.

## The truth

- The man calling himself Kane is **Emit**, an escaped prisoner from the future. He has done terrible things across history, and this night is his safe base.
- **Dr. Aldous Kane is real.** He's the short, bald man on the fourth floor who invents time travel tonight.
- **Emit needs time travel to be invented, but he needs to be the one controlling it.** He has been intercepting visitors who would become a problem for him later, and "dealing" with them.
- Emit's "cheap black watch" (its date reads 12.03.40) is **the Tether**, a stolen prison recall device tied to the first flash.
  - Whenever a hunter gets close to him, he pulls the whole night back to that moment. That's why letting an intruder through resets time.
  - The guard remembers each loop because he was standing nearest the first flash.

## True ending

- **The final shot:** you can win once you have three things: the wanted poster, the warden's file, and a look at the watch reading 12.03.40. You then shoot Emit the moment he runs in after the first flash. At that moment the Tether has nowhere earlier to send him, so the loop closes.
- **The epilogue, 6:12 AM:**
  1. The rain stops and morning comes.
  2. Warden Hask unbuckles the watch: *"Recall anchor. Prison issue, stolen... You were standing on the tablecloth."*
  3. He counts the marks scratched into your desk, which are your real number of resets.
  4. Then the real Dr. Aldous Kane comes down the stairs for the first time with a coffee, not knowing what you did: *"I think I actually made something."*
  5. The last line: *"Lightning, I'd told myself. It was never lightning."*

## Emit's endings (for reference)

1. **Stranded:** you help Emit stop time travel from being invented. He's stuck in the 90s, but nobody hunts him.
2. **Emit's guard:** you help Emit kill the real Dr. Kane. You end up guarding him as he takes over the world and time.
