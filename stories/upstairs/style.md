# Upstairs: art notes

On top of `/stories/ART-STYLE.md`. Story bible: `/story/upstairs/story-bible.md`.

- **Accent: candle violet** `#2e1e3a` `#6a4a8a` `#c8a8e8`. Only ever means *one of them*: the glint that crosses a fake's eyes every few seconds, the deputy's eyes under his hat, the attic candles in the Hollow Hill ending. Nothing real is ever violet.
- **The warm light is Owen's flashlight.** Before he finds it under the bed, only the nightlight. A dithered darkness mask (`darkness()` in `js/art.js`) is laid over every room, with a light pool for the flashlight, plus the room's own light where it has one (TV static in the living room, the lantern in the basement, the moon in the attic window).
- **Below the line is the floor at Owen's feet** (mostly under the text panels): his flashlight, the things he's carrying (flashlight, stepstool, photo pieces or the mended photo, attic pole, key), and his crayon notebook. In the bedroom it's his quilt; at bedtime Biscuit is asleep on it.
- **The fakes are mirror-wrong.** Draw them as the real person flipped where it counts: hair parted the other way, Dad's watch on the viewer's left (his right), the fake deputy's star on the wrong side and his number backwards (`digits(..., flip)`). Biscuit's fake collar is the one blue thing on him.
- **One house, one kit.** Every room is built from the same pieces in `js/art.js` (`door`, `casing`, `windowOn`, `curtains`, `crown`, `baseboard`, `boards`, `box`, `outlet`): six-panel doors 64×112 in the same casing, the back wall meeting the floor at y=146, oak boards running toward Owen. The Whitlocks' olive damask runs through the hall and the front hall; moving boxes sit in every room upstairs because it's the first night.
- **Two scales, never mixed.** Anything at the back wall is at door scale (Dad in Owen's doorway at bedtime is a whole person, 104 px tall). Anyone who comes up close to Owen is drawn big, and the furniture they're in is drawn at their size and stays that size when they're gone: Mom's rocking chair, Mom and Dad's bed and quilt (which runs off the bottom of the picture), Dad's wingback. People in the chairs get the chair's arms drawn back over them (`rockingChairArms`, `armchairFront`).
- **Rooms**: `bedtime`, `bedroom`, `hall`, `parents`, `bathroom`, `attic`, `foyer`, `living`, `kitchen`, `basement`, close-ups of the front and back door glass (`frontdoor`, `backdoor`; the kitchen door is glass nearly to the floor, so Mrs. Albright's bare feet show), and the `car` backdrop for the ending where Owen leaves with the deputy. Backdrops: `dad` (Dad in the doorway), `candles` (the attic circle lit, beech trees in the round window), `dawn` (the basement window going pink), `taken`, `black`, `car`.
- **Close-ups are 64×48** here, one game pixel to one canvas pixel, so a close-up reads as a nearer look at the same pixels. (The house rule is 32×24; this story doubled it for detail.)
- Story-only colours: `blue0` / `blue` / `blueHi` (Owen's stool, the fake collar, curtains, Dad's jeans), `pink` (Mrs. Albright, Biscuit's tongue), `gold` (Biscuit, badges), `olive0-2` (the Whitlocks' wallpaper), `rose0-2` (Mom and Dad's room and quilt).
